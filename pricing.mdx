## How data is counted

Per-GB plans and trials meter the bytes streamed to you. Fixed-price plans have no data cap.

- Data packs stack. Buy more at any time and the amounts add up.
- With several plans for the same product, usage draws from the plan that expires first.
- When an allowance runs out, new streams are refused with [`QUOTA_EXCEEDED`](/errors) and open streams end.
- Unused data expires with the plan.

## Paying

Pay in **USDC or USDT on Solana**.

- **Checkout.** Add plans to the basket, then pay by wallet (Phantom or Solflare), by scanning the Solana Pay QR code, or by sending the exact amount to the address shown.
- **Quotes.** A payment quote is valid for 30 minutes. The amount can differ from the list price by a few cents so the transfer can be matched to your order. Send the exact amount shown.
- **Activation.** A plan starts as soon as the payment is confirmed on-chain. Its 30 days run from then, including any time before you bind an IP to a fixed-price plan.

### Account balance

You can deposit to an account balance and pay from it.

- The first deposit is at least \$80; later top-ups are at least \$1.
- RPC plans and per-GB data are paid from the balance.
- If a transfer does not match an order, or pays more than it, the difference is credited to your balance.

If a payment arrives after its quote expired, email [support@sodae.io](mailto:support@sodae.io) with the transaction signature.
